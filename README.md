## Featured work

### [Accessibility Mapper](https://github.com/michaelcraft17/autism-community-resources) · [autismcommunityorg.org](https://autismcommunityorg.org/)
A free, interactive directory of **142,000+ verified autism and special-needs resources across 30+ countries**. Unlike Google or Apple Maps, nobody can add an unverified listing: every entry traces back to a government registry, a national health or disability database, or an organization's own published records.
`Python` · `data pipelines` · `web mapping`

### [Nexus](https://github.com/michaelcraft17/nexus-scenario-practice)
A judgment-free place for neurodivergent users to practice everyday social situations, like a vague request from a manager or small talk at a checkout. An AI plays the other person. The app is built on the idea that users shouldn't be coached to mask or "act normal." Built for the **IncludAI Neurodiversity Hackathon** (Track 2: AI for Connection & Wellbeing).
`JavaScript` · `LLM roleplay`

### [AI for Human Impact](https://github.com/michaelcraft17/ai-for-human-impact) · [live site](https://michaelcraft17.github.io/ai-for-human-impact/)
Website for *AI for Human Impact*, a BASIS Schools hackathon running Feb 6–12, 2027.
`HTML/CSS/JS`

### [Live Translator for Google Docs](https://github.com/michaelcraft17/live-translator-for-google-docs)
A Chrome extension that shows a live translation next to any Google Doc. Click a sentence in either pane and it is highlighted in both.
`JavaScript` · `Chrome Extensions API`

### [Tennis Recovery Robot](https://github.com/michaelcraft17/tennis_recovery_robot)
A Raspberry Pi robot on a mecanum-wheel chassis. It detects tennis balls with a trained computer-vision model and uses sonar to avoid obstacles (in progress, from my research at Cal Poly Pomona's SoftCom Lab, mentored by [@Sahagun](https://github.com/Sahagun)).
`Python` · `OpenCV` · `Raspberry Pi` · `hardware`
